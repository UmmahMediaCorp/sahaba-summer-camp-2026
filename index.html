Chapter One · Faith

Memorize Qur'an.

Tajweed in small groups. Hifdh tracks for every level — from the first juz' to consolidating what you've memorized.

Daily morning halaqas with qualified teachers. New readers, returning students, and active huffadh each get a track that meets them where they are — and pushes them where they want to go.

Chapter Two · Fun
02 · 06 Real → Anime

Run, kick, play.

Soccer, basketball, archery, swimming. An hour of movement built into every single day.

We rotate sports weekly so nobody gets bored, and we coach the soft skills too — sportsmanship, teamwork, how to lose with adab and win with shukr.

Chapter Three · Friends
03 · 06 Real → Anime

Find your people.

Brotherhood and sisterhood circles. The friendships that outlast the season — and outlast childhood, in shaa Allah.

Halaqas, group challenges, mealtime conversations. The unofficial highlight of every camper's summer is the inside jokes only camp creates.

Chapter Four · Adventure

Weekly field trips to Edmonton's best — Telus World of Science, Elk Island, swimming, paintball, surprise camp days.

Chapter Five · Worship
05 · 06 Real → Anime

Dhuhr and 'asr in jama'ah, every day, in our own masjid. Prayer becomes a habit when it's the rhythm of your summer.

Plus a Seerah halaqa, the lives of the Sahaba, and the fiqh of salah — taught with stories, games, and the rigor of qualified teachers.

Chapter Six · Craft
06 · 06 Real → Anime

Arabic calligraphy. Painting. Robotics. Photography. Every Friday a showcase — parents are invited.

The arts have always been a Muslim tradition. We honour that with intentional creative time built into every day, and dedicated workshops once a week.

For ages 11 — 15 · A new theme every week

Seven weeks. Seven journeys.

Every week of the camp has its own theme that ties together the Qur'an lesson, the halaqa, the activity, and the field trip — so the whole experience moves in one direction.

  1. 01

    Heroes of Islam

    The companions, the early heroes, the unsung — who they were, what they faced, and what we can carry forward.

  2. 02

    Kindness week

    Adab in action. Words, gestures, listening, helping. Daily challenges to bring kindness home.

  3. 03

    Sunnah Sports

    Swimming, archery, horseback — the sports the Prophet ﷺ encouraged, reframed for an Edmonton summer.

  4. 04

    Creativity week

    Calligraphy, painting, photography, simple engineering. Build something, present it, take it home.

  5. 05

    Leadership week

    How the Prophet ﷺ led. Public speaking, decision-making, leading a halaqa — each camper takes a turn.

  6. 06

    Around the Muslim world

    Andalusia, Mali, Indonesia, the Levant. Food, language, history — Muslim cultures around the globe.

  7. 07

    Healthy Muslim

    Nutrition, sleep, the body as an amanah. Cook together, move together, learn what the Prophet ﷺ taught about both.

Themes apply to our older campers (ages 11–15). Younger groups follow the daily schedule with age-appropriate adaptations of the weekly theme.
